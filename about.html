I competed as an NCAA Division I sprinter at UC Riverside while studying quantitative economics and econometrics. In campus recreation, I supervised student staff and coordinated games, facility capacity, and participant flow. I started using Excel and R to see where games were getting delayed and to give the team something concrete to work from when we changed coverage or schedules.

At Purdue, I bring that same mindset to decisions about customers, risk, and staffing. I want to know what a decision costs, what the people implementing it can control, and what evidence would make us change course. I’m pursuing roles in strategy and analytics, decision science, and operations analytics.
