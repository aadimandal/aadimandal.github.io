Business problem

Recurring late starts during busy recreation blocks made it harder to keep games on schedule. The operation needed to locate those delays and understand where staffing coverage, schedules, or league formats could help.

My role

As an operations manager at UC Riverside, I supervised student staff, coordinated evening recreation, and built Excel and R reports to make schedule and coverage issues visible. I recommended league-format changes and supported the team’s staffing and scheduling adjustments.

Method

I tracked participation, forfeits, incidents, staffing coverage, and schedule performance. The weekly reports surfaced repeated late starts, peak-window bottlenecks, and staffing gaps, giving the team a way to connect timing problems to coverage and league format.

Decision and outcome

The reports informed where the recreation operation adjusted staffing and schedules. Over two quarters, the operation reduced late game starts by 20% while maintaining 95%+ on-time game completion. These figures describe the operation’s aggregate performance during the changes.
